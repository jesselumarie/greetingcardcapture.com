Greeting Card Capture

Privacy Policy

For Greeting Card Capture 2.0 · Updated November 5, 2026

Greeting Card Capture is a Messy Industries LLC product. Contact us at support@greetingcardcapture.com with privacy questions.

Your cards

Your card images, sender names, occasions, dates, and notes are stored in the app on your device. The app does not send this content to the developer. It does not require an account with us, and version 2.0 includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics services. We do not sell your card data.

Camera and photo access

Camera access lets you photograph or scan a card. The system photo picker lets you choose images to add. When recovering cards from an older version, the app may request photo-library access to find the original images. The app does not delete your original Photos assets. You can change Camera and Photos permissions in your device's Settings; limiting access can prevent capture or recovery of some images.

iCloud and recovery

When iCloud backup is enabled and iCloud Drive is available for the app, it automatically copies card images, metadata, and saved library versions to your private iCloud storage. You can turn this off in the app's Settings using Back up to iCloud. Backups use a randomly generated identifier to keep each installation's saved versions separate. Recovery can also read your older Greeting Card Capture records in your private iCloud database. The developer does not receive these private backups or recovered cards.

Apple provides iCloud through your Apple Account under Apple's privacy policy. You can manage the app's iCloud access and stored data in Apple's device and account settings. Disabling access does not erase existing cloud copies. These are versioned backups, not live editing across devices.

Purchases

The optional full-version upgrade is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. The app uses Apple's StoreKit service to check and restore the upgrade, and stores its unlock status on your device. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy policy.

Sharing and support

Apart from the iCloud backups described above, you can choose to share images or ZIP backups. Exporting creates a ZIP in the app before you choose a sharing destination. ZIP backups are not encrypted by the app and may contain private photos, names, and notes. Choose a destination you trust; the destination's privacy practices apply to copies you send there.

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you include so we can respond. Please avoid sending private card images or notes unless they are needed for your request. Contact support to request deletion of information you have sent us; some correspondence may need to be retained for legal obligations.

Retention and deletion

Moving a card to Recently Deleted does not permanently erase it. The app retains deleted cards, earlier saved versions, and legacy recovery data so you can recover them; it does not automatically purge this history.

To remove all copies, separately manage the app's local data, iCloud data, exported backups, and original Photos images. Deleting the app removes its local app data, but does not remove independent exports or iCloud copies. Reinstalling or restoring a backup may bring saved cards back. Export an independent backup before removing anything you want to keep.

This website

Greetingcardcapture.com uses Google Tag Manager to load advertising tags, including the Pinterest tag and Meta (Facebook) pixel. These tags share website activity with Pinterest and Meta for advertising measurement, audience creation, and personalized advertising. This can include pages visited, links clicked, IP address, browser and device information, and cookie or similar identifiers. Our current Pinterest setup records page visits and clicks on App Store links; these clicks do not tell us whether you installed the app or made an in-app purchase.

Pinterest and Meta may combine this website activity with other information they hold to personalize ads and content under their own policies. Read Pinterest's privacy policy and Meta's privacy policy. The website also includes Google Analytics code; see Google's privacy policy for Google's practices.

You can manage advertising personalization in Pinterest's privacy and data settings and Meta's Ad preferences in Accounts Center, including Activity from other businesses where available. You can also use the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out tool for participating providers and manage cookies or tracking protection in your browser. These choices can limit personalization; they do not necessarily stop all data collection or all advertising. Cookie-based choices may need to be repeated for each browser or device and after clearing cookies.

These website tags are separate from the app. They are not included in Greeting Card Capture 2.0 and do not receive your app's card images, notes, or private iCloud backups.
